epic AI and the epic AI app

"epic AI" is the AI assistant on oeper.dev. It also comes as an Android app, which uses the same account and the same rules. You can use it without signing in, with a smaller daily allowance.

  • Daily limits. epic AI has a daily allowance counted in tokens (roughly, pieces of words that you send and receive). The allowance depends on whether you are signed out, signed in, or have a plan or code that raises it, and it can be changed, lowered or paused at any time, for example when the shared free capacity of the AI service runs out. Signed-out use is counted by network address.
  • Codes and product keys. A promo code or product key gives a bonus or unlocks a product such as oeper+. Codes have no cash value, cannot be sold or exchanged for money, and can be limited, expire, or be removed from accounts that abuse them or break these terms. Guessing codes, or sharing a single-use key so that others can use it, is not allowed.
  • Phone control. If you switch it on, the app can use Android's accessibility service to read what is on your screen and to tap, scroll and type for you when you ask it to do something. A bar with a Stop button is shown while it works, it asks before risky taps (send, buy, delete, confirm), it does not type passwords and it avoids banking, wallet and password apps. You are responsible for what you ask it to do and for checking the result. Turn it off in the app or in Android's accessibility settings whenever you like.
  • Memory file. If you are signed in, epic AI keeps a short memory file about you (and a list of quick facts) so it can help you better over time. It fills the file in from your chats, and it may occasionally ask you a question to get to know you. It is meant for lasting things such as what you like, what you study or do, and how you want answers written, and it is told not to store passwords, ID, card or bank numbers, exact addresses, or health, religion, sexuality or political details, or private details about other people. You can read and edit the file, delete anything in it, and switch learning off at any time, in epic AI's memory panel on the website and in the app's Settings. What you write in the file is treated as true, so do not put anything in it that you would not want epic AI to use when it answers. Whatever is in the file is sent to the AI service with each of your messages while you are signed in.
  • Pictures. If you are signed in, epic AI and the One suite can draw pictures from a description. There is a daily limit on how many you can make, which depends on your plan, and it can be changed or paused at any time. Your description is checked by an automated safety filter and sent to the AI service (Cloudflare Workers AI, and for the best-quality pictures, Google through Cloudflare); we can refuse a description. Pictures are made by AI, so they can be wrong or odd, may resemble existing things by accident, and are not checked by a person. You are responsible for how you use them, and you must not ask for pictures that break the rules above, such as sexual content with minors, harassment, or fake pictures of real people meant to mislead.
  • Shared conversations. If you are signed in you can share a conversation or an answer as a link. Anyone with the link can read the copy, so only share what you are happy for other people to see, and only what you have the right to share. You are responsible for what you share. Do not use shared links to spread anything illegal, abusive or misleading; links can be reported from the shared page and may be removed. You can stop sharing at any time and the copy is then deleted.
  • Connectors. You can add connectors (MCP servers) that epic AI can call. They are run by other people, not by us. What epic AI sends to a connector, and what it does there, is governed by that connector's own terms. Connectors can only change things if you allow it.
  • Answers can be wrong. epic AI can make mistakes, invent facts, or misread a screen. Check anything that matters before you act on it, and do not use it where a mistake could hurt someone.

You can send a bug report, an idea or a legal concern from the app (Settings, Help) or at oeper.dev/feedback.
