short version

oeper.dev does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other sites. It stores a small amount of data in your browser that the site needs in order to work.

third parties that see your browser

When a page loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts, Google receives your IP address. If you attach a PDF to epic AI, your browser also downloads the PDF reading library from jsDelivr, which receives your IP address too. No other advertising or analytics service is loaded on oeper.dev pages. A few tools hosted on the site (such as game or editor embeds) load their own libraries and may contact their own servers, see their pages for details.

consent

Because we only use strictly necessary storage and preferences you chose, the law in most places does not require a consent banner. We show a small notice once so you know this. If we ever add anything optional such as analytics, we will ask first, and you will be able to say no.

controlling it

You can clear site data in your browser settings at any time. If you do, you will be signed out and your saved preferences on this device will reset. Blocking all storage will stop sign-in from working.

More detail on the data we hold about you is in the privacy policy. Questions: sanhackerman@gmail.com.
