No speaker

A voice built from two tones, with no speech engine behind it. It wakes up, talks, sweeps through its range, sings, raps over a beat and lets you play it by touch. An app version of nospeaker.

before you start

  • Turn the volume down first: the high notes are piercing.
  • Keys: space skips a part, q goes to the end (twice to stop), tab types the suggestion in "say anything".
  • Touch to play: slide for pitch, move up for more vibrato, press harder for louder (pressure only on touch screens and pens).

what this is, and isn't

The original nospeaker makes a MacBook's trackpad motor buzz at up to 4,000 times a second so that the glass itself talks. Windows PCs and most other computers have no such motor, so this app plays the same sounds through your normal speakers. The voice is two tones restarted on every beat of the pitch, hiss sounds are bursts of high tones, and a small built-in rule set turns your text into sounds, so it mispronounces unusual words. It is robotic on purpose.
