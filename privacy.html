who we are

oeper.dev is a personal website run by an individual who goes by "oeper.dev". Anywhere this policy says "we", it means that person. You can reach us at sanhackerman@gmail.com.

what we collect and why

whatwhy
your google account name, email address and profile photo, received when you sign in with googleto create your account and show who you are to other people on the site. Your email address is also what your account is keyed by in our database.
profile details you add: handle, bio, badge and similarto show on your public profile.
things you post: posts, comments, stories, videos, projects, notes, uploaded files, direct and group messages, follows, reactionsto run the features you asked to use. We do not run advertising, we do not use analytics or tracking scripts, and we do not sell your data.

who can see it

  • the public: your handle, name, photo, bio, public posts, comments, files you made public and your public activity.
  • other users: only what you send them, such as messages and credit transfers.
  • the site owners: because we run the database and the file server, we can technically read stored content. We only look at private content when needed to handle a report, enforce the terms, fix a fault or comply with the law.

services that process data for us

We use these providers. Each receives the data needed for its job and has its own privacy policy.

Some of these providers are located outside your country, so your data can be processed in other countries, including the United States.

how long we keep it

  • Your account data and content stay until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Uploads made without an account expire automatically, and larger files expire sooner.
  • Deleted files move to a trash and are removed after a retention period.
  • Moderation and audit records may be kept for as long as needed to protect the site and other users.

your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to object to some processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. In practice:

  • You can edit your profile and delete your own posts, comments, files and messages from the site.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and the data tied to it, or to send you a copy of your data, by emailing sanhackerman@gmail.com from the email address on the account. We aim to reply within 30 days.
  • You can remove a signed-in device in settings, and you can revoke this site's access from your google account at any time.

Deleting something from the site does not always remove it from copies other people made, or from backups, straight away.

children

Sign-in uses a google account, and google's own age rules apply to having one. We do not knowingly want personal information from children who do not have permission to use the site. If you are under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live, use oeper.dev only with the permission and supervision of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can email sanhackerman@gmail.com to see, correct or delete a child's data, and we will act on a verified request quickly. Do not post personal details about yourself or others in public areas.

security

Connections to the site use HTTPS, and database access is limited by access rules. No online service can promise perfect security, so please do not share passwords or sensitive personal information in messages or posts.

changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, for large changes, post a notice on the site.
